The I-70 Bass Anglers holds monthly tournaments for active club members from March through November, fishing some of the best bass waters in Kansas.  

Boaters and Co-Anglers are paired up through a random drawing held at the meeting prior to the month's tournament and compete as a team. It does not matter who catches the fish, the points and winnings are awarded to the pair as a team.

Tournament results are tallied each month as members individually compete for Angler of the Year and Co-Angler of the Year.
The top Anglers and Co-Anglers qualify for the annual no-entry-fee Club Classic.

If you are looking to experience tournament bass fishing for the first time, this is the club to join. There are experienced club members within our Angler and Co-Angler rosters ready to help. If you are already experienced with tournament fishing, the I70 Bass Anglers would be excited to have you join and help grow the community of bass fishing along the I70 corridor.

### Tournament Information

- **Season:** March/April through October (8 tournaments + Club Classic)
- **Format:** One-day tournaments, safe light to 2:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** $50 per angler
- **Payout:** Based on number of boats

#### Rules Summary
- **Bag Limit:** 5 bass minimum 15 inches
- **Format:** Angler paired with Co-Angler
- **Check-in:** 15 minutes before start time
- **Weigh-in:** Typically 8 hours after launch

**Season-End Awards:**
- Angler of the Year (most points)
- Co-Angler of the Year
- Big Bass of the Year
- Newcomer of the Year

#### Weather Policy  
Tournaments proceed in most weather conditions. Cancellations or delays will be announced:
- **Via:** Email, club website, phone tree
- **Makeup:** Rescheduled at next club meeting, when possible
