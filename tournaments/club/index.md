### Tournament Information

- **Season:** March/April through October (8 tournaments + Club Classic)
- **Format:** One-day tournaments, safe light to 2:00 PM
- **Entry Fee:** $50 per angler
- **Payout:** Based on number of boats

#### Rules Summary
- **Bag Limit:** 5 bass minimum 15 inches
- **Format:** Angler paired with Co-Angler
- **Check-in:** 15 minutes before start time
- **Weigh-in:** 2:00 PM sharp

**Season-End Awards:**
- Angler of the Year (most points)
- Co-Angler of the Year
- Big Bass of the Year
- Newcomer of the Year

#### Weather Policy  
Tournaments proceed in most weather conditions. Cancellations or delays will be announced:
- **Via:** Email, club website, phone tree
- **Makeup:** Rescheduled at next club meeting, when possible
