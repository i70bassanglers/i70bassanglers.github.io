Who We Are

We’re a small but passionate group dedicated to competitive bass fishing and making sure the sport thrives for generations to come. Whether you’re a seasoned tournament angler or just getting started, you’re welcome on the water with us.

Season

  • March through October
Live Weigh-Ins Fish are weighed live in front of the crowd, then released. No paper tournaments. No apps.
Team Format Boaters and Co-Anglers compete together. The best 5 fish per boat count, regardless of who caught them.
Club Classic Season’s top finishers earn a spot in an end-of-year invitational — no entry fee.
Joining the Club
  1. 1
    Attend a Meeting Come out any first Thursday of the month — 7:00 PM at Acorns Resort.
  2. 2
    Fish a Tournament Compete in a club tournament as a guest to get a feel for how we fish.
  3. 3
    Member Vote Receive a majority vote from active members at the next meeting.
  4. 4
    Pay Dues & Fish Pay annual dues and start competing in the points race.
