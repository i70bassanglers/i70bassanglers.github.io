About the Series

The I-70 Bass Anglers holds monthly tournaments for active club members from March through November, fishing some of the best bass waters in Kansas. Boaters and Co-Anglers are paired through a random drawing held at the meeting before each tournament — it doesn’t matter who catches the fish, points and winnings go to the pair.

Tournament results are tallied each month as members compete individually for Angler of the Year and Co-Angler of the Year. The top finishers qualify for the annual no-entry-fee Club Classic.

New to tournament fishing? Experienced club members are ready to help you get on the water. Already experienced? We’d love to have you join and help grow the bass fishing community along the I-70 corridor.

Season-End Awards

  • Angler of the Year
  • Co-Angler of the Year
  • Big Bass of the Year
  • Newcomer of the Year

Scoring

  • 1st place: 100 pts
  • No fish weighed: 50 pts
  • Big bass bonus: +2 pts
  • Best 5 of 8 count
  • Meeting attendance: up to 4 pts
