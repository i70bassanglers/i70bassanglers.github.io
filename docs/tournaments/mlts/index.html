About the Series

The I-70 Bass Anglers proudly hosts the Milford Lake Team Series, a competitive bass fishing tournament series held on Milford Lake near Junction City in Northeast Kansas. Sign up as a team and compete throughout the season for cash — 90% of entry fees go straight back to the anglers.

MLTS tournaments are open to the public. Teams compete on Milford Lake, one of Kansas’s top bass fisheries, in single-day events throughout the spring and summer season. Individual tournament payouts are determined by the number of competing teams, with cash paid at the weigh-in.

At the end of the season, the overall Team of the Year is awarded $500 in addition to their tournament earnings. Contact the Tournament Director to get your team registered.
