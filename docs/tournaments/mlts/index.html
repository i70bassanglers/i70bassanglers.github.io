Milford Lake Team Series

The I-70 Bass Anglers proudly hosts the Milford Lake Team Series.

The MLTS is a competitive bass fishing tournament series held at Milford Lake near Junction City in Northeast Kansas with a 90% payout.
Sign up as a team and compete throughout the season on Kansas's premier fishing destination for cash!

Tournament Information

One-day tournaments held on Milford Lake.
8 hours of fishing from safe light.

Entry Fee: $100 per team.
Launch: South Boat Ramp, Google Maps

Payout Details

Individual Tournament Day winnings will be determined based on the number of teams competing.
Winning Teams are paid cash at the weigh-in.

The Team of the Year is awarded $500 at the end of the season.
